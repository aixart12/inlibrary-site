InLibrary

A study timer for exam aspirants

InLibrary helps you count the hours you actually study. Plan your subjects and topics, run focused study sessions against a daily goal, keep notes, look back at your history, and study alongside friends in shared libraries.

Your sessions, planner and notes stay on your phone. Signing in and the Library tab are optional.
