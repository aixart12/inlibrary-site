Features

Everything you need for exam prep, in one quiet app.

No feeds, no distractions. Just your plan, your timer and an honest record of the work you put in.

  • Focus timer

    Only real study time counts. A focus timer with a gentle break buzz every 25 minutes, with Diya keeping you company.

  • Syllabus planner

    Plan subjects and topics against an exam-day countdown, with today’s plan right on your home screen.

  • Notes for every subject

    Write quick notes, scan a page with your camera, or attach a photo, all tagged to the subject it belongs to.

  • History and streaks

    See every day you showed up on a heatmap, keep your streak alive, and check your hours by subject.

  • Diya’s lamp

    Earn oil for showing up, hitting your goal and finishing topics, then spend it to protect your streak on a missed day.

  • Study with friends

    Create or join a library, see who is studying right now, and compare this week’s hours, if you want to.
